About Me

I'm Sumeer is a passionate photographer with over 7 years of experience, specializing in automotive photography. His keen eye for detail and dynamic compositions have made him a go-to photographer for capturing the essence of vehicles. Alongside his automotive work, Sumeer also enjoys photographing various events, from intimate gatherings to grand occasions, bringing his expertise and creativity to every shot. Whether it’s the sleek lines of a car or the candid moments of a celebration, Sumeer’s photography tells a unique story through every frame.
